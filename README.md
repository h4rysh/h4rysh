### ✦ About Me

I am a software engineer focused on building **smart, reliable systems** at the intersection of AI, financial security, and assistive product engineering. I design systems that combine non-deterministic LLM intelligence with deterministic verification rules to prevent errors and protect vulnerable users.

- 🎓 **Education:** B.Tech in Information Technology, Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology
- 🎯 **Current Focus:** AI-driven financial reconciliation, scam-resistant payment flows, and offline-first Android care systems.
- ⚡ **Engineering Principle:** *"Strict validation over raw model outputs. Systems must fail safely and transparently."*
